# Individual Reflection — Solo CampusHelp Project

Write in your own words, and update after the project:

1. What did I personally plan and implement?
2. Which GitHub project management features did I use?
3. Which tasks were most difficult and how did I resolve them?
4. What do actual Issues, commits, branches, PRs and test results show about my work?
5. What would I improve in a second iteration?

**Do not claim peer reviews or other team members.** Note the lecturer approved individual completion of the normally group-based assignment.
